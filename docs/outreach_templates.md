## 🎥 2. The 60-Second Loom Video Audit Script

Record a quick screen capture video with your face bubble in the corner. Keep it strictly under 60 seconds.

* **0:00 - 0:15 (The Problem)**: Show their current website on a simulated phone screen (Chrome DevTools mobile view). Point out 1–2 specific flaws (e.g., text overlapping, missing click-to-call button, non-HTTPS warning).
* **0:15 - 0:40 (The Solution)**: Switch tabs to your customized demo preview (generated via `scripts/generate_demo.py`). Show the instant click-to-call header, the 30-second quote calculator widget, and Google rating badge.
* **0:40 - 0:60 (The CTA)**: *"If you'd like to check out the full preview link or talk about putting this live for your business, reply to my email or give me a quick call!"*

## 🛡️ 4. Objection & Pricing Handling Playbook (Discovery Call)

When they reply *"How much does this cost?"*, present your 3 tiers:

### Tier Pitch:
* **Basic Tier ($150/mo + $300 setup)**: Simple 1-3 page mobile site, 24/7 click-to-call, Google Maps setup, SSL, hosting, and quarterly updates.
* **Standard Tier ($300/mo + $500 setup)**: Multi-page custom site, interactive quote calculator, Google Analytics 4 tracking, and monthly performance reports.
* **Premium Tier ($600+/mo + $1,000 setup)**: Full e-commerce, custom CRM/booking integration, active SEO blogging, and API connections.

### Objection: *"Why should I pay monthly instead of a one-time fee?"*
* **Response:** *"Traditional agencies charge $3,000 to $5,000 upfront and then charge $100 per hour every time you need business hours changed, a new photo added, or when plugins break. With my $150/mo plan, your hosting, SSL security, quarterly copy updates, and local SEO schema are 100% included with zero surprise bills."*
