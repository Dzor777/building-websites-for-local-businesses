# Linkless 2-Step Outreach Playbook
### High-Deliverability Permission Messaging for Texas Contractors (Zero Calls, Zero Cold SMS)

---

## 1. Why "Linkless Message #1" Wins

Cold emails and direct messages often get flagged as spam when they include links, domain redirects, or tracking pixels on the very first touch. 

By removing **all links** from Message #1:
1. **Near 100% Inbox Placement**: Spam filters (Gmail, Outlook, Yahoo) classify plain text without URLs as authentic peer-to-peer personal emails.
2. **Curiosity & Hand-Raise**: Asking a simple low-friction question (*"Mind if I send you a quick preview?"*) triggers natural curiosity without triggering defense mechanisms.
3. **Whitelisted Delivery**: Once the contractor replies (*"Sure"*, *"What is it?"*, or *"Send it over"*), their email provider automatically whitelists your address. Message #2 with your demo link is guaranteed to land in their primary inbox.

---

## 2. The 4 Channels for Linkless Outreach

| Channel | Method | Finding Contacts | Deliverability / Open Rate |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Direct Email** | Plain text email directly to owner/company inbox | Google Maps listings, website footers, Facebook "About" email | **High (Primary Inbox)** |
| **2. Website Contact Forms** | Submitting short linkless message via their existing form | Any contractor with an old/broken website | **100% Inbox Delivery** (Bypasses spam filters completely) |
| **3. Instagram DMs** | Direct message to business profile (0 links) | Search `#[city]plumber`, `#[city]roofing`, `#[city]painter` | **High** (DMs from public accounts are open) |
| **4. Craigslist Email Relay** | "Reply by Email" on contractor service ads | Craigslist Dallas/Fort Worth `Services -> Skilled Trades` | **100% Delivery** (Routed through Craigslist relay) |

2. **Find 5 Contractors on Craigslist Skilled Trades** (`Dallas / Fort Worth`).
   - Click "Reply" and send **Craigslist Relay Message** (0 links).
3. **Find 5 Visual Contractors on Instagram** (`#dfwpainter`, `#dallastreeservice`, `#fortworthlandscaping`).
   - Send **Instagram DM 1** (0 links).
4. **Respond to Inquiries**: As soon as someone replies "Sure" or "Send it", reply within 15 minutes with **Message 2** containing the matching demo link.
