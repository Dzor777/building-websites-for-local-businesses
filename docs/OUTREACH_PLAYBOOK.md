## 🎥 3. The 60-Second Loom Video Audit Script

Record a quick screen capture video with your face bubble in the corner. Keep it strictly under 60 seconds.

* **0:00 - 0:15 (The Problem)**: Show their current website on a simulated phone screen (Chrome DevTools mobile view). Point out 1–2 specific flaws (e.g., text overlapping, missing click-to-call button, non-HTTPS warning).
* **0:15 - 0:40 (The Solution)**: Switch tabs to your customized demo preview (`https://dzor777.github.io/building-websites-for-local-businesses/?client={slug}`). Show the instant click-to-call header, the interactive quote calculator widget, and 24/7 dispatch form.
* **0:40 - 0:60 (The CTA)**: *"If you'd like to check out the full preview link or talk about putting this live for your business, reply to my email or give me a quick call!"*

## 💡 Strategic Rules: When to Pitch Add-On Services

| Add-On Product | Best Sales Timing | Conversion Rate |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Logo Polish ($300 One-Time)** | **Checkout Email** - Mentioned as a 1-line optional add-on when they say "Yes!" | ~30% Conversion |
| **Google Review Booster ($100/mo)** | **Day 14 After Site Launch** - Pitched as the next step to double Google Map Pack calls | ~45% Conversion |

> ⚠️ **CRITICAL RULE:** **NEVER pitch add-ons in Cold Email #1.** Cold outreach must focus strictly on 1 single mobile pain point. Pitching multiple products in cold emails causes decision paralysis.
