## 1. Executive Summary & Strategy Shift

Cold email can be slow and burdened by spam filters. Local blue-collar contractors (HVAC techs, roofers, plumbers, electricians, painters, tree services, lawn care operators, pressure washers) run their businesses on their smartphones and spend substantial daily time in:
- **Facebook Local Contractor & Trade Groups** (e.g., *DFW Contractors Network*, *Texas Roofing & Trades*, *Local Lawn Care Pros*)
- **Facebook Business Pages** (where their contact info is directly listed)
- **Community Business Groups** (e.g., *McKinney Small Business Exchange*, *Plano/Frisco Local Services*)
- **Inbound "ISO Web Designer" Posts** (homeowners or business owners asking for recommendations)

This playbook outlines battle-tested scripts and messaging protocols using our **8 production-grade interactive trade demos**.

## 3. Communication Guidelines (Human Tone vs. Corporate Speak)

Contractors immediately tune out corporate agency jargon. Follow these rules on every platform:

- ❌ **Avoid**: "We specialize in end-to-end digital transformation and conversion-rate optimization."
- ✅ **Use**: "I build fast mobile sites for Texas contractors that make it dead simple for homeowners to call you or get an instant quote."
- ❌ **Avoid**: Em-dashes (`—`), buzzwords like "synergy", "paradigm", or long corporate walls of text.
- ✅ **Use**: Short sentences, commas, periods, conversational local tone. Sign off with "Thank you," instead of formal agency sign-offs.
- ❌ **Avoid**: Generic mockups with blank gray boxes or stock generic office buildings.
- ✅ **Use**: Direct trade links with matched trade photos and working estimate tools.

## 6. Common Objections & Bulletproof Responses

### Objection 1: "I get all my work from word of mouth."
> "Word of mouth is always king in trades. But even when a neighbor recommends you, the homeowner still Googles your name on their phone before calling. If they see a broken page or no website, they often hesitate and call the next guy. A clean mobile site just makes sure your referral calls actually dial your number."

### Objection 2: "Can't my nephew or a cheap builder on Fiverr do this for $100?"
> "They definitely can build a page. The difference is they don't specialize in local trades. Most cheap sites take 8 seconds to load, don't work on mobile thumb zones, and break within two months. With us, your site is hosted on enterprise cloud servers, includes your quote tool, and anytime you need a price or phone number changed, you just shoot me a text."

### Objection 3: "Am I locked into a long contract?"
> "Zero contracts. Everything is month-to-month. If you ever want to cancel, you can do so at any time. We keep clients by doing good work, not by locking anyone into contracts."

### Objection 4: "What if I need custom features or extra pages later?"
> "Standard monthly plans cover regular content and maintenance updates. Any out of scope updates or large custom features are always available for a discussed and negotiated additional fee, so there are never any surprises."
